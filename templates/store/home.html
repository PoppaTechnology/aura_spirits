Our Heritage

Generations of Craftsmanship

Founded in 1924, Aura Spirits began with a simple philosophy: never compromise on quality. For nearly a century, our master distillers have passed down techniques that balance traditional copper alembic methods with modern innovation.

Every bottle we produce is a testament to patience. From selecting the finest organic grains and mountain botanicals to the final maturation process in hand-selected European oak casks, we believe that true perfection takes time.
